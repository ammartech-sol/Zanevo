U-Net Segmentation
Plant Disease Segmentation

Identify visible disease regions on a plant leaf photo using a U-Net trained from scratch, pixel by pixel, not just a single sick/healthy label.

Trained from scratch

No pretrained backbone. 4-stage encoder, bottleneck, 4-stage decoder with skip connections. Every layer built directly in PyTorch on the PlantSeg dataset.

Model stats
75% whole-image classification accuracy on held-out validation
11,000+ images from PlantSeg (Kaggle / Zenodo)
15 classes across 15 plant species, chosen by sample size and visual diversity
Input fixed at 256×256, images resized before inference
What makes it different
Pixel-level mask output, not a yes/no label
Combined cross-entropy + Dice loss; every class improved after the switch
Mask bug caught by raw pixel inspection, not symptoms. Values were 4-114, not 0/255
Exported to ONNX and verified against PyTorch output before deployment
Known limitation

Apple scab lesions cover a median of ~6% of the image vs 40%+ for the strongest class. Small, sparse targets are harder for a fixed-architecture segmentation model. Explainable, not a bug.
