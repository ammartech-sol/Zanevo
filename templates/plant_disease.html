U-Net Segmentation

Plant DiseaseSegmentation

Identify visible disease regions on a plant leaf photo using a U-Net trained from scratch, pixel by pixel, not just a single sick/healthy label.

Trained from scratch

No pretrained backbone. Every encoder/decoder layer was built and trained directly on the PlantSeg dataset.

What the model does
Segments the exact diseased region, not just a yes/no label
Covers 15 disease classes across 15 plant species
Trained with combined cross-entropy + Dice loss for better region overlap
75% whole-image classification accuracy on held-out validation
Known limitation

Apple scab detection is weaker than other classes since its lesions cover a much smaller share of the leaf, a harder target for segmentation, not a bug.
