Customer Segmentation

Segments customers into three behaviorally distinct groups using RFM analysis and K-Means clustering. Built on 83,246 customers from the Olist Brazilian e-commerce platform.

Auto-Computed Features

Recency is calculated automatically from the date you enter. You don't need to compute days yourself, just pick the date of the last purchase.

What the model evaluates
Last order date → recency in days since purchase
Total lifetime spend → monetary value (USD)
Log transform applied to both before clustering
Frequency dropped — 90%+ of customers had only 1 order
K=3 selected via Elbow + Silhouette (score 0.4129)
Possible segments
Active Customers
Purchased recently (~40 days avg). Nurture and upsell.
Lapsed High-Value
High historical spend (~$138), inactive ~278 days. Win-back candidate.
Lost Cheap
Low spend (~$38), inactive ~281 days. Low reactivation priority.
Trained on the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset (83,246 customers). Segmentation reflects behavioural patterns from 2016–2018 transaction data. Results are indicative, not prescriptive.
